01

Get started with your cat

Home → Get started

Start with one thing you want to organize. You do not need to fill in every feature or use the app every day.

How to use it

  1. Open solocat.click, choose English or Thai, then sign in with Google or start without an account.
  2. Guest records stay in that browser. For cloud backup, sign in with Google and check the backup status in Profile.
  3. Home has Tasks, Income, Expenses, OT and Home. More contains Savings, Financial summary, vehicles, Emergency and Lazy Cat.
  4. Use the bottom + button to add an entry and Calendar to review dated entries. Check the title, date and amount after saving.
  5. If your browser offers Install app or Add to Home Screen, use it for convenient access.
Example

Add one salary entry and one rent entry, then open Financial summary to see their effect.

Good to know

Google sign-in is separate from the contact email typed in your profile. A typed email does not verify account ownership.

02

Tasks, Calendar and repeating entries

Tasks / Calendar / +

Keep appointments and due dates together and see what is complete or still waiting.

How to use it

  1. Open Tasks and add a task, or use +. Choose an entry type and a recognizable title.
  2. Set its date, time and details. For repeating entries, choose a frequency and review the upcoming occurrences before saving.
  3. Open Calendar and select a day. If an entry is missing, check the month, date and filters.
  4. Open the details and mark a task complete. Mark expenses paid only after actually paying.
  5. Before editing or deleting a repeating entry, check whether the change applies to this occurrence or the series.
Example

Create a monthly air-conditioner cleaning task and mark each occurrence complete when the work is done.

Good to know

A payment button or paid status records your action. It does not transfer money or collect payment.

03

Income: record money received

Home → Income or More → Income

Use actual receipt dates so monthly totals reflect the money you recorded.

How to use it

  1. Open Income, select the month and choose Add income.
  2. Enter a title such as salary, extra work or a sale, plus the amount and date received.
  3. After saving, check the month matching the entry date, not only the current month.
  4. Open an entry to edit or delete a mistake, then check the total again.
  5. Use Financial summary to compare income with paid expenses and the balance brought forward.
Example

If you receive 20,000 baht on the 25th, record that receipt date when tracking money actually received.

Good to know

OT and extra-work earnings are separate from Income. Record received money in Income to include it in Financial summary, and avoid entering it twice.

04

Expenses and recurring subscriptions

Home → Expenses

Review monthly commitments and separate paid amounts from outstanding entries.

How to use it

  1. Choose a month and review its total, paid and unpaid amounts.
  2. Choose Add an expense. Select a popular preset or enter your own title.
  3. Set the date, frequency, time, category and amount. Match recurring bills to their billing frequency.
  4. After saving, use View all for the full list and filter by all, unpaid or paid.
  5. Mark each entry paid after payment. Check the selected occurrence or series scope before editing or deleting a recurring bill.
Example

For 5,000 baht rent due on the 1st, create a monthly entry and mark each occurrence paid after payment.

Good to know

Category cards summarize amounts; use View all to browse entries. A monthly budget is a planning reference, not your bank balance.

05

Debt: separate principal and interest

Add expense → Debt

Track outstanding principal using recorded payments, without treating interest as principal repayment.

How to use it

  1. Choose Debt and enter the initial principal outstanding before this series of records.
  2. Enter principal paid and interest paid separately. Use 0 for either if paying only the other part.
  3. For installments, choose the frequency and due date you want to record.
  4. After paying, check the principal and interest for that occurrence and mark it paid.
  5. Review remaining principal. Once it reaches zero, new occurrences stop while paid history remains.
Example

With 10,000 baht initial principal, a payment of 1,000 principal plus 100 interest records a 1,100 expense and leaves 9,000 principal after marking it paid.

Good to know

Older entries without initial principal cannot stop automatically until configured. The app uses your entries, not a lender’s balance feed.

06

Savings and a retirement target

More → Savings

Record both deposits and withdrawals so current savings reflect your entries.

How to use it

  1. Choose to add savings, then enter the amount, date and supported details.
  2. When using savings, choose withdrawal and record the amount taken out.
  3. Review the history. Edit or delete mistakes rather than adding duplicates to hide errors.
  4. Set a retirement savings target and planned monthly contribution to see progress and an estimated time to the target.
  5. Deposits and withdrawals update both the balance and target estimate.
Example

Adding 3,000 baht and withdrawing 500 baht gives a net savings increase of 2,500 baht.

Good to know

Savings are separate from the monthly balance and are not automatically deducted from Income. Target dates are estimates from your plan, not a guarantee of retirement readiness.

07

Understand Financial summary

More → Financial summary

Review income, expenses, remaining money and savings without confusing their meanings.

How to use it

  1. Choose the month you want to review.
  2. Balance brought forward is earlier income minus earlier expenses marked paid.
  3. Monthly balance equals brought-forward balance plus this month’s income minus this month’s paid expenses.
  4. Review the amount after unpaid expenses to estimate what remains if all pending entries are paid.
  5. Current savings are separate. Editing old entries also changes calculated carryover for later months.
Example

2,000 brought forward + 20,000 income − 12,000 paid = 10,000 remaining. Another 3,000 unpaid leaves a projected 7,000 baht.

Good to know

These totals use Solo Cat entries, not bank data. Savings show the current total even when reviewing an older month and are not counted again as income.

08

Overtime, daily work and extra jobs

Home → OT

Overtime uses hours and pay rates. Extra jobs use the agreed job amount. Choose the matching mode.

How to use it

  1. Open OT settings and choose hourly, daily or monthly pay, including the inputs for conversion to an hourly rate.
  2. Set the cutoff and payday. For twice-monthly periods, configure both cutoff/payday pairs separately.
  3. For OT, enter the date, hours, hourly rate and multiplier. The default multiplier is 1.5; adjust it to your work arrangement.
  4. For daily work or extra jobs, use the separate tab and enter the date, hours and job payment without applying the OT multiplier again.
  5. Configure holidays in their separate menu. Review the summary with OT, extra work or all records and the appropriate month/year.
Example

2 OT hours × 100 baht base rate × 1.5 = 300 baht. For a 3-hour extra job agreed at 800 total, record a job payment of 800.

Good to know

Check your actual pay arrangement. The app does not confirm employer payroll. Existing entries retain their saved pay policy; changing settings does not rewrite every past amount.

Home, family and people you care for

Home → Home

Organize information around individual people, with separate home and document records.

How to use it

  1. Select an existing person or add someone you care for, such as a partner, sibling or grandparent.
  2. Enter their name, choose a relationship and an available picture, then save.
  3. Open their page before adding related records so they belong to the intended person.
  4. Use Home & assets or Important documents for titles, details and necessary attachments.
  5. Check the person’s name before editing or deleting, especially when names are similar.
Example

Add “Grandma” and keep her related records on her page, separate from your parents’ information.

Good to know

Adding a member on your device does not send an invitation or automatically give them access to your data. Emergency sharing is separate.

Car and motorbike records

More → My car / My motorbike

Keep maintenance dates, insurance, tax reminders and documents together.

How to use it

  1. Open the vehicle area and use its options to edit the name, model/year and plate.
  2. Add a care entry with its title, due date, maintenance/insurance/tax type and notes.
  3. Overview shows unfinished entries. Mark an entry complete after the work is actually done.
  4. History shows completed care. Use Documents to add or view document records.
  5. Check file size before attaching a document and reopen it to verify the correct file.
Example

Add “Renew car insurance” with its due date, then mark it complete after renewal.

Good to know

The app does not renew insurance, pay tax or book service appointments on your behalf.

11

Lazy Cat: rest, small wins and dreams

More → Lazy Cat

Make space for rest, small achievements and personal goals at your own pace.

How to use it

  1. Choose activities, achievements or dreams.
  2. Use the activity options to find ideas matching your time, budget and mood.
  3. Add something you actually completed to achievements and revisit its history for encouragement.
  4. Create a dream with a title and target amount. Open it to add contributions and review progress.
  5. When your goal changes, edit the existing dream instead of accidentally duplicating it.
Example

Create a “Weekend away” dream with a 10,000 baht target and record contributions toward it.

Good to know

Dream contributions are records for that goal, not transfers. They are not automatically linked to the main Savings balance.

12

Emergency information, contacts and sharing

More → Emergency / Important contacts

Keep useful details close at hand and share only the fields you choose.

How to use it

  1. Select the country where you need the service. Check the country and number before calling; interface language does not select the country automatically.
  2. Edit your name, emergency contact, phone, blood group, health details, allergies, medicines or notes as you choose.
  3. SOS opens service choices. Selecting a number opens your device’s dialer; you still need to place the call.
  4. To share, sign in with Google, expand sharing, select specific fields and give consent before creating a link.
  5. Send the link to your intended recipient. Anyone with the link who signs in with Google can view the selected fields. Links expire after 7 days.
  6. Turn sharing off to revoke access, or create a new link to update the snapshot and invalidate the old link. Add frequent contacts in Important contacts.
Example

To share only a name and contact phone, select those two fields. Unselected health details are not included.

Good to know

Local edits do not update a shared snapshot until published. Revocation cannot retrieve screenshots or copies recipients already saved. SOS does not automatically dispatch help.

13

How your information is protected

Data security

Cloud backup records and attachments use AES-256-GCM encryption, so stored files are not directly readable as personal information.

Protection and its scope

  1. Cloud storage: records and files are encrypted before storage. The decryption key is held separately from the backup files. Transfers use HTTPS.
  2. Account access: the backup service checks your Google account before serving its records. Knowing a file link does not grant access.
  3. Scope: this is server-side encryption. An authorized server can decrypt information to display or restore it for you. It is not end-to-end encryption or a claim that access is impossible in every circumstance.
  4. On-device data: working records are stored in your browser. There is no dedicated app lock, and browser data does not have the same protection as encrypted cloud objects. Use a screen lock and private browser profile.
  5. Downloaded backups: the file is encrypted using your chosen password. You need it to restore the file; the service cannot recover that password.
Example

A stored cloud backup object contains encrypted content. After the owner signs in, the authorized app can display the information normally.

Good to know

Selected emergency fields are readable by recipients under the link’s access conditions. Older recovery copies may remain under the existing 7-day recovery policy. Encryption does not replace device and account protection.

14

Backup, restore, attachments and offline use

Profile → Backup and restore

Check that important information is actually backed up, rather than only present on the device.

How to use it

  1. Sign in with Google, open Backup and restore, and check the successful cloud backup status and timestamp.
  2. Offline, use supported parts already loaded on your device. Changes wait for connectivity. New sign-in, cloud downloads and publishing shared links need a connection.
  3. New attachments are limited to 2 MB each and 20 MB of active attachments per account. Reduce or remove unwanted files if over the limit, then check backup status.
  4. For your own copy, choose a password of at least 12 characters and download the .solocat file. Confirm it was saved and keep the password separately.
  5. To restore, choose the file, enter its password, review its details and type the on-screen confirmation before replacing data.
  6. Before clearing site data, moving devices or switching accounts, verify backup success and keep a downloaded copy. Preserve a backup before resolving conflicts.
Example

After recording work offline, reconnect and check the latest backup timestamp before clearing or retiring the old device.

Good to know

Cache helps open previously loaded content; it does not guarantee every page works offline and is not cloud backup. File restore replaces current records. Older attachments above new limits are not automatically deleted.

15

Notifications, language and updates

Profile → Notifications / Settings

Configure your device and choose when to refresh the app.

How to use it

  1. Choose your language in Profile. It changes interface text, not the titles you entered yourself.
  2. Open Notifications, follow browser permission steps and check that device notifications are enabled.
  3. For missing alerts, check site permissions, system settings, Do Not Disturb and connectivity.
  4. For the latest version, save your work and use Profile → Settings → Refresh app.
  5. If records look wrong, check your account, month, filters and backup status first. Do not start by clearing site data.
Example

If an updated screen still looks old, use Refresh app instead of deleting browser data.

Good to know

Delivery depends on the browser and operating system. Do not rely on notifications as the only channel for emergencies or critical deadlines.

16

Storage and optional statistics

Profile → Settings

Choose whether to allow statistics while continuing to use the app.

How to use it

  1. Essential storage supports sign-in, your records, offline use and backups. These features do not require statistics consent.
  2. On first opening the app, choose Essential only or Allow statistics. Statistics do not start before you allow them.
  3. Optional statistics include Google Analytics usage data and member return counts. Return counts send activity dates for the signed-in account, not the contents of your personal records.
  4. Change your choice in Profile → Settings. This choice applies to this browser; other devices have separate choices.
  5. Withdrawing stops new statistics and clears queued activity dates on this device. It does not delete your records, backups or statistics already sent.
Example

Essential only still lets you record tasks, income and expenses and back up after signing in.

Good to know

Backup does not publish your information. Sharing emergency information with others requires a separate sharing choice

Solo Cat guest statistics use a random persistent browser ID, first visit timestamp, a limited source/campaign tag, and Guest start, first saved item and new signup milestones. They never send task, expense or note contents, and do not collect country. Visible online visits count once per browser per day; queued offline milestones count on the server receipt date. Counts represent browsers, not verified people. The ID lasts until site storage is cleared or statistics consent is withdrawn. Detailed reports are owner-only. Backup encryption is unchanged.

17

My Pets: pet care

More → Page 2 → My Pets

For signed-in Google members, with a separate profile and history for each pet.

How to use it

  1. Add a name, species, breed, birthday, sex, weight and important notes such as allergies.
  2. Open Profile to add up to 3 JPG, PNG or WebP photos per pet, up to 2 MB each within your account’s 20 MB file quota.
  3. Record meals, water and treats with dates, times, quantities and completion status.
  4. Record vaccines, medicine, deworming, baths, haircuts, nail care and vet visits, including next dates.
  5. Keep weight, mood, stool, urine and health notes in each pet’s history.
  6. Review monthly expenses by category and save vet or animal-hospital contacts with tap-to-call links.
Example

Select a pet before adding records. Photos and care history remain separate. Edit a record to change or delete it.

Good to know

Pet expenses are separate from your main finances. Schedules appear in the overview; pet push reminders are not enabled. Check backup status before changing devices

Common questions

Must I fill in everything first?

No. Start with one task or expense and add other features when useful.

Does the app connect to my bank or pay bills?

No. Totals come from your entries. Marking an entry paid only updates its recorded status.

Does encryption mean nobody can ever read the data?

No. Cloud objects are not directly readable, but an authorized server can decrypt them for the owner. Local and deliberately shared data have different protection boundaries.

How do I move to another device?

Check backup success on the old device, sign in with the same Google account on the new device, and verify your records. Keep a separate encrypted file backup before clearing the old device.

Will offline work disappear?

Saved records remain in that browser and wait for backup. Clearing site data or using a non-persistent browsing mode before backup can lose local records.
